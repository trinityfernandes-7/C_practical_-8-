Algorithm:
Step 1: Start
Step 2: Declare variables for Employee Name, Employee ID, Basic Salary, HRA, DA, PF, Gross Salary and Net Salary.
Step 3: Accept Employee Name from the user.
Step 4: Accept Employee ID from the user.
Step 5: Accept Basic Salary from the user.
Step 6: Calculate HRA as 20% of Basic Salary.
Step 7: Calculate DA as 10% of Basic Salary.
Step 8: Calculate PF as 12% of Basic Salary.
Step 9: Calculate Gross Salary by adding Basic Salary, HRA and DA.
Step 10: Calculate Net Salary by subtracting PF from Gross Salary.
Step 11: Display the Employee Salary Slip containing Employee Name, Employee ID, Basic Salary, HRA, DA, PF, Gross Salary and Net Salary.
Step 12: Stop
